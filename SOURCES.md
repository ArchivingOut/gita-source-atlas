# Sources, collection guide, and rights register

**Archive policy:** preserve a citable source snapshot where reuse is permitted; otherwise provide a carefully described link to the source steward. A catalogue listing is not a manuscript transcription, and a portal link is not permission to mirror its content.

## Preserved primary text snapshot

- **The Gita Initiative**, repository `gita/gita`, upstream files `data/verse.json` and `data/chapters.json`: https://github.com/gita/gita
- Declared upstream license: [Unlicense](https://github.com/gita/gita/blob/main/LICENSE).
- Snapshot preserved in [data/corpus.json](data/corpus.json), retrieved 2026-11-09. It contains 701 source records. Chapter 13 includes a separately numbered Arjuna question; editions differ in how it is numbered, and common descriptions cite 700 verses.
- The archive preserves source text, supplied transliteration, and supplied word meanings. Those glosses are not an independent translation or validated Sanskrit morphological analysis.
- The website runs exact substring search over the source fields. The frequency visualization counts tokens from the supplied English gloss field, not Sanskrit lemmas. The chapter chart counts records. The concept network is a curated editorial navigation layer, labeled as such.

## Comparative editions, commentaries, translations, and recitation

- **Heritage IIT Kanpur / Gītā Supersite** — https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/gitasupersite — comparative verse readings, multiple scripts, commentaries, translations, word analysis, and recitation. The host describes it as continuing the IIT Kanpur Supersite and records that texts remain associated with their respective source stewards. This archive links out; it does not mirror those materials.
- **IIT Kanpur verse access** — https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/SBG/2.47 — example verse route; replace reference to explore. Rights and editorial choices belong to each text's steward.
- **GRETIL (Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages)** — https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/ — machine-readable Indological e-texts; its index lists Mahābhārata: Bhagavad-Gītā material. Consult individual text headers and terms before reuse.
- **Gītā Initiative** — https://github.com/gita/gita — preserved base dataset and its source license.
- **Sanskrit Computational Linguistics / University of Hyderabad GOLD data** — https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/scl/GOLD_DATA/ and its [tagged-data index](https://sanskrit.uohyd.ac.in/scl/GOLD_DATA/tagged_data.html) — external part-of-speech, dependency, compound (samāsa), and sandhi resources are linked from the Text Lab. These resources can include texts beyond the Gītā. Check each package’s inventory and rights. The lab does not copy or conflate these annotations with our base transcription.
- **Gītā Supersite resource guide** — https://heritage.iitk.ac.in/resources — additional corpus and Sanskrit tool pointers.
- **Internet Archive Gītā recitation search** — https://archive.org/search?query=Bhagavad%20Gita%20recitation — results vary; verify the item-level creator, rights, and recording metadata.
- **Holy Bhagavad Gita verse pages** — https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/47/en/ — per-verse source page linked for hosted recitation access. Audio remains at the host.

## Manuscript and catalogue discovery

These are research gateways or catalogue-level finding aids, not claims that this project has inspected or digitized every Gītā witness:

- **Union Catalogue of Corpus of Indian Knowledge (UCCIK)** — https://www.uccik.in/ — searchable union manuscript catalogue initiative.
- **Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA), manuscript catalogue resources** — https://ignca.gov.in/online-digital-resources/manuscripts/ — catalogue access; identify the collection and shelfmark for a specific witness.
- **UNESCO Memory of the World nomination dossier, five selected Śāradā Gītā manuscripts** — https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/mow001/bhagavadgita-five-select-manuscripts-in-sarada-script.pdf — describes selected witnesses and reports access conditions; consult the dossier and holding institution before citation or reuse.
- **Asia and Pacific Museum digital manuscript catalogue, Bhagavad Gītā (Mahābhārata), MAP 21728** — https://manuskrypty.muzeumazji.pl/en-manuscript-21728/ — one catalogue object and its metadata; not a comprehensive census.
- **Gauḍīya Grantha Mandira Sanskrit repository via IGNCA** — https://ignca.gov.in/online-digital-resources/gaudiya-grantha-mandira-sanskrit-text-repository/ — catalogued root-text resource.

A witness-level archival record should include repository, shelfmark/catalogue ID, script/material, date estimate as catalogued, folio extent, digitization/access URL, rights/access statement, and whether the displayed text is a scan, diplomatic transcription, normalized e-text, or edition.

## Audio and rights

Audio is not rehosted. Per-verse links and directory searches point to outside services; availability and rights vary by recording. Browser speech synthesis uses the selected transcription on-device and is not a traditional recording. Follow the terms attached to each specific item.

Modern editions, translations, commentaries, scans, recordings, and databases can remain protected even when the underlying Sanskrit work is ancient. This repository's MIT license covers project code only. The corpus file retains its upstream Unlicense attribution. The rights of linked material are not changed by linking.
