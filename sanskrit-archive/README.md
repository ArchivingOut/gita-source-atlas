# Sanskrit Archive

A classroom-ready interactive story map for Sheldon Pollock’s Chapter 1, “Sanskrit Literary Culture from the Inside Out,” in *Literary Cultures in History*.

## Open the published story map

Once the repository’s Pages workflow finishes, open:

https://archivingout.github.io/gita-source-atlas/sanskrit-archive/

## Use the map

- Choose **Story** and use Next/Previous (or the arrow keys) to present the chapter in narrative order.
- Select a journey layer to explore one route. Click a globe dot or a location card to read its note and chapter reference.
- Choose **Reference map** to show the supplied overview. The 3D globe uses an online globe library and Earth texture; if they are unavailable, the map image remains available.
- The full read-aloud class script is in `PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md`.

## Source and interpretation

The chapter PDF is the primary source. The supplied presentation informed the story sequence; `assets/map_reference.png` is the overview-map reference. Location notes include chapter page references where possible. Coordinates are approximate reading aids, not claims of exact ancient locations or travel roads. Alakā is literary and mythic geography.

The 114 plotted stops follow the chapter’s named journeys and cultural centres. They are an interpretive guide, not an archaeological survey or a claim that every place name in the chapter has a secure modern equivalent.

The map adds a spatial comparison to Pollock’s argument: Sanskrit literary culture was a mobile learned cosmopolitan practice rather than a single fixed place or only a body of sacred texts.
